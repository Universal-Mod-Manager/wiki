To select a plug-in in UMM:

1. Open UMM.
2. Go to the plug-in list.
3. Click the plug-in you want to use.
4. Check its details, requirements, and compatibility.
5. Confirm that it matches your game version and setup.

If multiple plug-ins do the same thing, pick the one that is maintained, compatible, and clearly documented.

You can also change mod priority with **drag & drop** or by editing the **priority numbers** if plug-ins conflict.
